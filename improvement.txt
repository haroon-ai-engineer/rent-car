Highest Impact Additions

Add real pricing blocks.
Show “Starting from AED …” for top 3 services with what is included/excluded. Hidden pricing often reduces inquiries.

Add a quick quote form above the fold.
Fields: Name, Phone, Pickup, Drop, Temp range, Date/time. Keep WhatsApp button too, but form captures leads you can follow up.

Add proof of trust.
Show compliance badges and evidence:
HACCP, temperature logs, sanitized fleet, insured vehicles, GPS tracking, years in business.

Add real customer proof.
Use real client names/logos (if allowed), before-after outcomes, delivery stats (on-time %, spoilage reduction).

Add service area clarity.
One clean section:
“Primary: Abu Dhabi” and “Also serving: Dubai, Sharjah, Al Ain, RAK.”
Unclear service coverage causes drop-offs.

Add response promise.
Example: “Quote within 5 minutes on WhatsApp.”
A concrete SLA improves conversion.

Things To Remove or Reduce (can hurt business)

Remove any fake-looking claims.
If a number is not verifiable (e.g., 100+ daily deliveries), either prove it or soften it.

Remove repeated/duplicate service cards.
Too many similar cards can feel spammy and lower trust.

Remove inconsistent wording.
Mixed terms like “Whatsapp/WhatsApp,” “Rental Hire,” or city focus switching (Dubai vs Abu Dhabi) weakens professionalism.

Remove dead navigation items.
If “Blog” doesn’t have real posts, either create posts or remove/rename to “Resources.”

Reduce keyword stuffing in long paragraphs.
Write naturally for humans first. Over-optimized text can hurt credibility and SEO quality signals.

Conversion Improvements

Add sticky “Get Quote” bar on mobile (single CTA).
Add “Call now” business hours and after-hours policy.
Add FAQ focused on objections:
pricing, minimum hours, same-day availability, temperature guarantee, cancellation terms.
Operational Improvements (business side)

Add CRM tracking for every lead source (WhatsApp, call, form, referral).
Add basic KPIs:
lead-to-booking %, average response time, repeat customer rate.
Add post-delivery review request flow (automated).
